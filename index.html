How to play

  • Place exactly one dog in every row, every column and every colour.
  • Dogs need their space: they can't touch, not even corner to corner.
  • If a puzzle has no easy way in, it starts with one dog already in place to get you going.
  • 👆Tap a square to mark ✕ (no dog). Double-tap to place or remove a dog.
  • 👉Drag across squares to mark lots of ✕s at once. Start a drag on a ✕ to rub them out.
  • A dog in the right spot stays put. A dog in the wrong spot cracks a bone and turns into a red ✕. Lose all three bones and it's a do-over.
  • Logic Hint explains the next step and highlights it. Apply does it for you, and Undo takes it back.
  • Find a Dog places one dog. Sniff Out crosses out three empty squares. Each tool works 3 times per puzzle. Reach level 21 to unlock a daily puzzle.
